The Yahoo Finance piece headlined *"Wall Street has a new way to chase the AI memory boom"* underscores the central investment thesis: **AI infrastructure buildout is creating unprecedented demand for DRAM memory chips.** Large language models (LLMs), inference engines, and AI accelerators (like GPUs and TPUs) are extremely memory-bandwidth-intensive, which is creating a structural supercycle demand for high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and advanced DRAM.

Key beneficiaries in the space include companies like SK Hynix, Samsung, and Micron (MU), which are the primary producers of HBM3 and HBM4. An ETF like `DRAM` provides concentrated, thematic exposure to this trend, which is evidently resonating strongly with investors.
