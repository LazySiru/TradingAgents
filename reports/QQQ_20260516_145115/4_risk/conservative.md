The macro picture compounds every technical concern I've raised, and this is where I part ways most firmly with any hold argument. Both CPI and PPI printed hot in the same week. This is not noise. And QQQ is sitting at a 34.58 times earnings multiple. Let me be very precise about what that means in a rising rate environment. When the risk-free rate rises, the denominator in every discounted cash flow model expands, and the present value of future earnings falls mechanically. QQQ's premium valuation is not supported by current earnings alone — it is supported by the market's expectation of substantial future earnings growth discounted at relatively accommodative rates. Hot inflation data directly threatens that assumption. If the Fed is forced to maintain a hawkish posture or, worse, signals additional tightening, the multiple compression on a 34.58 P/E instrument could be severe and rapid. A 10 percent multiple compression alone, without any change in underlying earnings, would move QQQ from roughly $712 toward $640. Combined with the technical mean-reversion dynamics already in play, the downside scenario is not a tail risk — it's a plausible base case.
